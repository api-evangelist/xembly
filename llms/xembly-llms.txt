# Xembly

> Xembly was a Seattle-based enterprise AI company (2020-2024) whose AI assistant, Xena, acted as an automated "chief of staff" for knowledge workers — scheduling meetings, taking meeting notes, and tracking action items and tasks over email and Slack. Spun out of Madrona Venture Labs by Pete Christothoulou (CEO, Marchex co-founder), Jason Flaks (CTO), and Peter Francis (CGO), and backed by Lightspeed Venture Partners (seed, 2021), Norwest Venture Partners ($15M Series A), and Seven Peaks Ventures. Xembly abruptly discontinued its customer-facing services on June 1, 2024, teasing a "new chapter" that has not publicly materialized. The company never published a public developer API, SDKs, or developer documentation; its product surface was the hosted Xena assistant plus calendar/Slack/Salesforce integrations.
